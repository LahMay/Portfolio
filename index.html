Why?

You may be wondering why I'm going the route I'm going, learning programming and animation. The answer is the simple fact that I want to play games.

I'm a migrant from Thailand and have live a relatively low technology life. When I was expose to the wonders of the internet, especially games, I wanted more. But being relatively poor, I didn't have the luxury of playing those top games.

It was at my young age that I decided that I want to be rich so I can play all that games. But as I grow up, I realize one fundamental thing, the games of today can't keep up with my imagination.

Watching videos and reading novels, my perception of games were shrewd into the realm of those only possible in the future. An example is Virtual Reality Gaming, where the game is well simulated as if you were there.

Thus was my change objective from getting rich to making it big in the gaming industry. I planned to graduate and contribute to the advancement of gaming, creating wondrous games my childhood self imagine to be real.
